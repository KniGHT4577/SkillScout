Please manually configure the Render Dashboard for your services as it is overriding `render.yaml` and detecting this as a Rust project:

1. Go to your Render Dashboard
2. Select your web service
3. Go to Settings -> Build & Deploy
4. Update the "Root Directory" to `backend` (or `frontend` for the UI service).
5. Update Language to `Python` (or `Node` for frontend).
